# Roadmap

This roadmap captures the direction of loxitui, the terminal UI for
[LoxiLB](https://github.com/loxilb-io/loxilb). Items are intent, not
commitments, and priorities shift with community feedback.

## Near term

- First functional release: browse load-balancer rules, endpoints, and
  connection-tracking entries of a LoxiLB instance.

## Exploring

- Live metrics views and rule editing.

## How to influence this roadmap

Open an issue or start a discussion describing your workflow.
